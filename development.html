1️⃣ 五维拆解法

形·拼写development＝develop + ment：词尾 -ment 别忘带
✏️ 口诀：先发展（develop）再结壳（-ment）
音·发音/dɪˈveləpmənt/ 重音在第二音节 vel
🎧 跟读：with the development of... · the development of ancient civilizations
意·释义n. 发展；壮大——课本表格原话：黄河帮助古代文明的发展
用·句型① Yellow River helped in the development of ancient civilizations. ② With the development of science, our life changes fast. ③ The development of the town brought more visitors.
拓·同族develop v. 发展 · developing / developed adj. 发展中的/发达的 · envelope n. 信封（同根趣词）

📋 功能句总结 · develop → development；with the development of...

动词：Rivers helped ancient civilizations to develop. 名词：Rivers helped in the development of ancient civilizations. 写作金句：With the development of..., ...（随着……的发展） 词性链：develop v. → development n. → developing / developed adj.

📝 中考考点卡 · development

考点1　with the development of... 随着……的发展（写作高频句型）
考点2　developed country 发达国家 ↔ developing country 发展中国家（形容词易混）
易错　development 拼写带 -ment；develop 的过去式 developed；help in the development of... 介词 in 固定

⚖️ 易混辨析卡 · 别再认错development
developed ↔ developing
国家搭配一字之差：developed country 发达国家，developing country 发展中国家
developedadj. 发达的：a developed country
developingadj. 发展中的：a developing country
develop ↔ development
动词与名词换挡：develop 去 -ment 动起来
developv. 发展：Rivers helped civilizations to develop.
developmentn. 发展：the development of science
🔥 火眼金睛：选词填空：China is a ______ country with a long history.
“发展中国家”是 developing country（现在分词表“正在发展”）；developed country 是“发达国家”。两词一字之差，方向不同。

📖 微型故事《The Elevator Question》

班会辩论“古镇要不要装观光电梯”。正方摆数据：电梯带来游客、带动收入。反方程橙放了张老照片：“我爸说，他小时候的青石板，现在只剩半条了。”自由讨论时有人提议：修步道、装木扶手——钱少，味道还在。班主任总结：发展不是把旧的拆掉，而是让新的学会绕路。程橙在周记里写：Development that erases memory is just demolition.抹掉记忆的发展，只是拆迁。教室后排，有人悄悄把“绕路”两个字写进了自己的笔记。

🧑‍🏫 自测提问卡：
• 细节题：What did Cheng Cheng show during the debate?
• 推断题：Why did someone write “绕路” in their notes?
• 迁移题（开放）：In your town, what should be developed and what should be kept? 用英语写两句

🗺️ 思维导图

┌── 核心 ── development n. 发展；壮大（develop + ment） ├── 句型链 ── with the development of... · help in the development of ├── 词性链 ── develop v. → development n. → developing / developed adj. ├── 国别链 ── developed country 发达 ↔ developing country 发展中 └── 课本链 ── the Yellow River → the development of ancient civilizations（p34）

🏛️ 词源故事

develop 经古法语 desveloper 追到两块词根：des-（除去）+ veloper（包裹）——本义“解开包裹”，引申为“展开、发展”。它的反义词藏在信封 envelope 里：en-（往里）+ veloper＝“包进去”。一个解开、一个包上，两词共用同一个动词——发展与守护，原是一体两面。
